# Wood you Dye 3.0.0

Wood you Dye 3.0.0 is a total redesign of Wood you Dye. Adding everything from Dyed Logs and Stripped Logs, to Dyed Nether and Bamboo sets!

I've worked really hard on this update, trying to make it really get up to the standards I hold for myself now, but if theres any issues feel free to report them!

### Additions
#### New Sets
- Nether Sets
  - Yes, you can get actually dark wood. Shocker I know but it finally happened.
- Bamboo Sets
  - Thought it would fit nicely.
  
#### New Blocks/Items
- Logs
  - Finally have logs, really only added for EveryCompat support.
- Boats
    - BOATS AFTER 3 YEARS!?!??! WHAT!?

### Changes
- Textures have been COMPLETELY overhauled.
  - Keep in mind the old Programmer Art pack is out of date!
- Dyeing planks/logs now accepts 8 of them.
  - No need for a constant companion any more!
